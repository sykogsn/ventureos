## Product development (parallel, on v1.0)

These are **product** programmes on the locked OS. They are not Foundation amendments.

1. Deepen Qualora as an incubating product through Venture Definitions and shared capabilities.
2. Keep Calviora and Farmora honest about excluded features until identity and atmosphere programmes land.
3. Admit further products only through the Definition Registry ([Future products](../../foundation-library/06-PRODUCTS/Future/README.md)).

## Reserved, empty by design

Capability classifications AI, Security, Communication, and Infrastructure may gain catalogue entries without changing Runtime authority. Empty is not dispatch.

## Explicitly out of scope forever unless Constitution is amended

- A second intelligence orchestrator
- A Product Registry
- Capability plugins that load or persist
- Persist as a Runtime stage
- A third climate
- Product-private navigation, type, or spacing systems
