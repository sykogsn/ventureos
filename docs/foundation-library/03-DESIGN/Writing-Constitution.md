Address the founder. Do not address an anonymous user.

## Rank of language

- Kicker names the desk or the room.
- Heading names the screen.
- Lead carries the one statement the screen exists to make.
- Body explains.
- Label names a control.
- Caption is supporting metadata.
- Code is an identifier, never briefing prose.

## Allowed tone

Calm, specific, operational. Missing intelligence is explained. “This floor is unseated” is correct. “No items” is not.

Prefer:

- Found, operate, decide, brief, desk, company, judgement
- Return to the Situation Room
- Found Company

Avoid:

- Generic “Loading…” as page copy (use VentureOS language or a skeleton)
- “No items”, “Oops”, “Welcome aboard”
- Dashboard, widget, magic, unlock, supercharge

## Empty states

An empty desk is a decision waiting to be taken. Name the next act. Do not apologise for an empty table.

## Errors

Fail visibly. Say what happened and what the founder can do. Do not leak whether an email exists on forgot-password. Do not use danger colour for emphasis.

## Product copy

Qualora may speak of quality, evidence, and findings. Calviora may speak of cadence and constraint using the **live** definition (livestock), not the unresolved EAS-001 healthcare proposal. Farmora may speak of field health and land. None of them rewrite OS chrome labels (Situation Room, Company HQ, Executive Office) unless a definition hides that room.
