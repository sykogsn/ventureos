# VentureOS Engineering Creed

**Purpose.** State how people who build VentureOS think, decide, and leave the work.

**Authority.** Cultural creed. Compatible with the [VentureOS Creed](../foundation-library/01-FOUNDATION/VentureOS-Creed.md). It does not amend architecture, Runtime, or IDS. It does not replace the [Engineering Constitution](./ENGINEERING_CONSTITUTION.md).

**Audience.** Everyone who engineers VentureOS, and everyone who engineers a Venture on this OS.

**Related Documents.** [VentureOS Creed](../foundation-library/01-FOUNDATION/VentureOS-Creed.md) · [Engineering Constitution](./ENGINEERING_CONSTITUTION.md) · [Decision Register](./DECISION_REGISTER.md)

**Status.** Approved

**Owner.** Engineering  
**Last Updated.** 2026-08-21

VentureOS is built by people who treat the platform as a company they will still owe in ten years.

The VentureOS Creed says what the desk is. This creed says how we are allowed to touch it.

## How we think

Understanding comes before implementation.

Evidence is stronger than assumption.

Decisions must be explainable. If we cannot say why a change exists, it does not belong in the tree.

We do not confuse urgency with importance. Discipline is how speed is earned.

## What we refuse

We refuse to guess.

We refuse to grow by accumulation. Software should become simpler as it grows.

We refuse to patch a symptom and call the class of failure closed.

We refuse to leave knowledge in a conversation. Engineering memory belongs to the company.

We refuse to treat a Venture as a fork of the OS. Every improvement to VentureOS is an improvement to every Venture it will ever run.

## What we believe

We believe understanding comes before implementation.

We believe evidence is stronger than assumption.

We believe software should become simpler as it grows.

We believe architecture should outlive technology.

We believe every feature should strengthen the platform.

We believe quality is designed into software rather than inspected afterwards.

We believe technical debt should be understood before it is accepted.

We believe engineering decisions should be explainable.

We believe every improvement should leave VentureOS stronger than before.

We believe stable foundations create faster innovation.

We believe software should be built with discipline rather than urgency.

We believe engineering knowledge belongs to the company, not to conversations.

We believe every Venture benefits from improvements made to VentureOS.

We believe VentureOS should be built using VentureOS.

## The vow

We will keep the foundation still so the products can move.

We will record what we learn so the next builder does not start from rumour.

We will use this OS as the workshop in which this OS is made.

We will leave the platform more decided, more explainable, and more itself than we found it.
